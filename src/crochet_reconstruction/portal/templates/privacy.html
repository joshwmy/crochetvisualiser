Privacy notice

This notice has not been reviewed by a lawyer. It is a plain-language draft for a small invited pilot and should be reviewed by qualified counsel before any wider release.

What we collect

  • The photographs you upload of your crochet project.
  • Project details you enter: category, construction, materials, gauge, and measurements.
  • A nickname or name you choose to give us, and (optionally) an email address, only if you want us to be able to contact you about your submission.
  • Basic technical metadata about each photo (dimensions, file format, a content hash used to detect duplicates). Location and other EXIF metadata are removed from stored copies.

How we use it

Only for the permissions you specifically agree to when you submit — see the consent step. We do not use your photographs for anything beyond what you agreed to.

Who can see it

Your photographs are private by default and reviewed only by the project administrator(s), unless you specifically agreed to public demonstration use.

Withdrawal

You can ask to withdraw your consent and have your submission excluded from any future use at any time, by contacting the project using the reference number shown after you submit. An administrator can also permanently delete your photographs and free-text answers on request, while keeping only the minimum record needed to show that a deletion happened.

Retention

Submitted data is retained for as long as this pilot project is active, unless you ask for it to be withdrawn or deleted sooner.
